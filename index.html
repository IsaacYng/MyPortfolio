Poem — English

The Gossip Rope

I am not upset with this cursed world, But something is growing in my forehead. That is not a dream, that is a peace. I see a rope, I see a hill, Which help each other to climb. I fear to sleep, I fear to dream, 'Cause this is a coffin. I want to be alert, I want to take care, But someone forces me to fall asleep, Someone forces me to see a dream. I see the calm, I feel the pain, My heart is burned Please pull me some, 'Cause this feels like an exit button. Oh, it is a dream, oh, it is a fear, Oh, it is a gossip rope. Sometimes I feel, it's not a curse, It's not a dream, it's not a force, O it's a gossip rope. I want to go back, I want to see a dream, I want to go back to my father's house, I worry about being The Prodigal Son.

Self-Realization
